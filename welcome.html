⏯️

Space Bar Play / Pause

Press Space (or 'k') to pause or play Shorts and Reels cleanly, safely suppressing browser page scrolling.

🔁

Hands-Free Auto-Scroll

Click the Auto button (positioned natively on the left for Reels and in the sidebar for Shorts) to auto-advance to the next video.

⏱️

Instagram Timeline Scrubber

Hover your mouse over any Instagram Reel to reveal an interactive time bar. Click or drag to seek anywhere effortlessly, even without signing in!

⚡

Playback Speed Controls

Press > or < (or Shift + . / ,) to fine-tune playback speed from 0.25x to 2.0x with on-screen HUD feedback.

⏩

Customizable Seeking

Use ← → arrow keys to skip backward or forward with your preferred duration (2s, 5s, 10s, 15s) set in the popup.

⏮️

Restart from 0:00

Press '0' to instantly restart the current Short or Reel from the beginning.

🔇

Volume & Mute Controls

Press 'm' to toggle mute/unmute, and use Shift + ↑ / ↓ to smoothly adjust audio volume.

🔄

Convert to Standard Video

Press Ctrl+Shift+F (Mac: Cmd+Shift+F) or click Convert to open Shorts in regular desktop player format.
